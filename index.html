I build websites that feel considered — where every pixel earns its place, and every interaction tells a story.

I'm Shaghik — a creative web designer and front-end developer obsessed with the details that separate good websites from unforgettable ones.

My work lives at the intersection of design and engineering. I specialize in Shopify stores, custom front-end builds, and brand websites for founders who care about craft.

I don't do templates. I don't do generic. I build digital experiences that feel like the brand — not like every other brand.
